# Vision

PaperWarden replaces risky online document utilities with a trustworthy local
workbench. A person should be able to open, inspect, convert, scan, OCR, sanitize,
protect, and export common documents while their device is in airplane mode.

## PaperWarden 1.0

Version 1.0 is a polished viewer, converter, PDF toolbox, scanner, OCR tool, and
privacy/security inspector. It is not a full Office editor.

## Success

- Core operations are dependable on supported Android and iOS devices.
- Every advertised format capability has corpus evidence.
- Malformed or hostile files fail safely.
- Processing leaves no unintended temporary data behind.
- The project operates with no recurring production infrastructure cost.
